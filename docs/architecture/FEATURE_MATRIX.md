# BrickVault V2 feature matrix

**M0 and M1 are accepted. M2 and M3 are in progress.** D03 leaves production release classifications provisional until the targeted workflow review and owner decision. The Architect manages routine checkpoints. Major decisions follow D08. [Master plan](MASTER_PLAN.md) · [Decision register](DECISIONS.md) · [Inventory and BrickLink contract](Inventory-and-BrickLink.md)

**How to use the register.** The section sets the target milestone. Dependencies name prerequisite capabilities. Overlapping implementation is possible while acceptance waits for the relevant evidence. *Mandatory* gates its milestone. *Optional* requires a recorded opt-in and an agreed gate if promoted. *Deferred* is proposed for later reconsideration. *Excluded* denotes a stated owner boundary or a proposed exclusion as identified in the disposition table. Status uses the owner's lifecycle (Planned, In Progress, Built, Reviewed, Accepted, Blocked, Deferred). The latter also records excluded work because the separate scope column carries the exclusion. An optional item marked Planned has a planned decision point and no implementation authorization.

**Acceptance evidence.** Each row states the evidence needed. Record M0 evidence against this public repository and its current checks. Keep private supporting material in restricted storage. The right-hand cells specify the required proof. Before marking Accepted, record the issue, reviewed commit and PR, version/environment, actual measured result, evidence location, recovery/failure result and any required owner decision against the ID. Reuse accepted evidence where scope matches. Keep fixture, database, hardware, provider and production evidence identifiable. Documentary V1 findings and Bricqer demonstrations support design. They cannot advance V2 implementation status. [Legacy assessment](Legacy-assessment.md) · [Bricqer workflow evidence](Bricqer-guide-review.md)

The first receiving workflow covers split and mixed deliveries, actual-bin confirmation, per-delivery cost coverage and explicitly backed unpublished offers. The qualification compares these records after restart and encrypted restore. The Locations inspector shows receipt layers for a selected bin across purchases, including whole-delivery cost evidence and current backing. Bin transfers maintain each receipt layer and its selected offer allocations and appear in both bins’ movement history. [Transfer checkpoint](https://github.com/abrianbaker80/BrickVault-V2/issues/18) The Inventory register searches physical positions across bins and retains each position’s receipt, currency and movement trail. For I05 and I06, a witnessed count preserves the observed and ledger quantities, opens a shortage review and prevents new use of disputed stock. A project can reserve identified units from a receipt position. During a shortage an operator selects the hold and exact quantity to release. The shortage review shows the effect a proposed loss would have on build reservations and unpublished offer backing. Posting an identified loss records its physical quantity, receipt-layer cost and the operator’s exact reductions to affected unpublished offers. Bulk recount freezes either selected positions or all filter matches and records per-target outcomes and witness links. For I02 and I06, post-putaway quarantine records pieces that remain physically in a bin while unavailable for sale. An inspected release identifies the original quarantine record and the quantity cleared. For I07 and I08, an operator can withdraw a chosen quantity of draft backing at a specific receipt position. A zero-backed offer keeps its original identity and price. [Voluntary withdrawal](https://github.com/abrianbaker80/BrickVault-V2/issues/35)
